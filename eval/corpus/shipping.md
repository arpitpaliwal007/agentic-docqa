# Northstar Devices Shipping Policy

## Service regions and schedules
Policy SHIP-Z4 covers deliveries in the continental United States. Orders placed
before 14:00 UTC on a business day dispatch the same business day if every item is
in stock. Orders placed later dispatch the next business day. Business days exclude
weekends and Northstar's published warehouse holidays. A dispatch estimate describes
when the warehouse hands the parcel to the carrier, rather than its arrival date.

## Shipping methods
Standard shipping costs $6 and arrives in 4-6 business days after dispatch. Express
shipping costs $18 and arrives in 1-2 business days after dispatch. Express delivery
requires a street address; it is unavailable for PO boxes. Selecting Express does
not shorten the warehouse's order cutoff or move an out-of-stock item into stock.
Standard shipping is free on orders with a merchandise subtotal of at least $75.
The qualifying subtotal is measured after discounts and before taxes and shipping.

## Tracking and delayed parcels
A tracking link is emailed after dispatch. Tracking may take 24 hours to show the
first carrier scan. If a parcel has no tracking movement for 5 business days, contact
support with the order number and tracking code. Support opens a carrier inquiry;
an inquiry does not automatically authorize a replacement or refund. A parcel marked
delivered but missing should be reported within 48 hours of the delivery scan.

## Address changes and grouped orders
An address can be changed through support only before dispatch. Once the parcel is
with the carrier, Northstar cannot guarantee rerouting. Orders containing a preorder
item ship together when all items are available unless support has approved a split.
For a split shipment, the customer receives a tracking code for each parcel. An order
may have several boxes even when it dispatches on one day. Shipping charges for a
standard approved split are not collected twice. These rules do not describe customs
duties or delivery services outside the stated region. A return shipment follows
the refund policy rather than the outbound delivery estimates in this document.
