# Northstar Devices Plan Pricing

## Available plans
The Basic plan costs $0 per month and includes 2 workspace members and 3 connected
devices. The Plus plan costs $12 per month and includes 5 workspace members and 20
connected devices. The Team plan costs $29 per month and includes 15 workspace members
and 100 connected devices. Member limits include the account owner. Device limits
apply across the workspace, rather than separately for each member. Prices in this
policy are stated in USD before applicable taxes.

## Annual billing and upgrades
Annual Plus costs $120 per year. Annual Team costs $290 per year. Annual billing
is paid in advance, and the feature limits are the same as the corresponding monthly
plan. An upgrade takes effect immediately; the unused portion of the current paid
period is credited toward the upgrade. That credit cannot be transferred to a
different account. Changes to workspace members do not automatically change plans.

## Trials and cancellation
Offer code TRIAL-PLUS-14 provides a 14-day Plus trial to a new account. No payment
card is required to start this trial. At the end of a trial without a purchased plan,
the workspace returns to Basic. A paid plan can be canceled in Billing settings.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. The owner
retains paid features until that period ends. A mid-period cancellation does not
produce a prorated cash refund. Returning a physical device does not cancel a plan.

## Usage above a limit
If a workspace returns to Basic with more than its allowed devices, existing devices
remain visible but new devices cannot be added until usage is within the limit.
The owner may remove devices or buy a plan with a higher limit. Northstar does not
automatically charge an overage fee or upgrade a workspace because its member count
or device count exceeds a plan limit. These plan prices do not include physical
devices, repair services, or outbound parcel delivery. For device returns consult
the separate refund policy, and for delivery charges consult the shipping policy.
