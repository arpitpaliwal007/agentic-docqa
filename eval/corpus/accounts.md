# Northstar Devices Account Policy

## Account access
Every Northstar account uses a verified email address. Password reset links expire
after 20 minutes. Requesting another link invalidates earlier unused reset links.
Support never asks a customer to disclose a password or recovery code. An account
owner can change an email address after verifying both the old and new addresses.
A failed email delivery does not automatically close the account or cancel a plan.

## Shared workspaces
An account owner may invite workspace members by email. The owner controls billing
and membership. Members may view devices assigned to the workspace but cannot change
the owner's payment method. The number of included members depends on the plan in
the pricing policy. Removing a workspace member revokes that member's workspace
access; it does not delete the member's separate Northstar account.

## Export and deletion
An owner can request a JSON account export through Settings > Privacy > Export.
The export download link remains valid for 72 hours. Exports include device names,
workspace membership, and saved automation rules. Payment card numbers are excluded.
An account deletion request has a 14-day cancellation period before deletion becomes
final. Signing in during that period does not cancel deletion: the owner must select
Cancel deletion in Privacy settings. Download needed exports before final deletion.

## Recovery and security notifications
Account recovery code ACC-REC-8 consists of 8 single-use codes. Each recovery code
works once, and generating a new set invalidates all remaining codes from the old
set. Keep recovery codes somewhere private outside the device used to sign in.
Security notifications are emailed when an account password changes or a new owner
email address is confirmed. Workspace invitations must be accepted using the email
address that received them. An account's deletion and a device warranty claim are
separate processes: a customer should retain proof of purchase independently of
account exports. This policy does not specify telephone support opening hours.
