# Northstar Devices Refund Policy

## Standard returns
Policy REF-30 applies to Northstar Devices orders purchased directly from our shop.
Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery. The return window
starts on the carrier's recorded delivery date, rather than the order date. Opened
devices are eligible if the device, charger, and original accessories are included.
Original packaging is helpful but is not required. A receipt or order number is
required. Support cannot approve a standard return without proof of purchase.

## Condition and exceptions
The Aurora Hub A2 may be returned under REF-30 even after activation. Devices with
accidental liquid damage are not eligible for a standard return. Personalized
engraved accessories are nonreturnable unless they arrived damaged or incorrect.
A delivery-damage claim must be submitted within 7 calendar days of delivery and
include a photograph of the damage. Damage claims are reviewed separately from
ordinary change-of-mind returns. Gift recipients need the original order number.

## Refund handling
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days
after warehouse inspection. A bank may need additional time to display the credit.
Northstar does not promise a date for the bank's own processing. Standard outbound
shipping fees are not refunded for change-of-mind returns. Northstar pays return
shipping for an item that arrived defective, damaged, or different from the order.
For an ordinary change-of-mind return, the customer pays return shipping.

## Request procedure
Use the support portal and select Returns, then provide the order number, device
serial number, and reason for return. Support issues a return authorization before
the parcel is sent. Parcels without authorization may be held until the sender
provides the missing details. The warehouse inspects accessories and device condition
before approving payment. A return does not automatically cancel an account plan;
plan cancellation follows the separate pricing policy. Store credit is available
only when the customer explicitly chooses it instead of an original-method refund.
