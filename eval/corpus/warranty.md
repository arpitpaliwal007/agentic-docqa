# Northstar Devices Limited Warranty

## Covered products
Warranty code WRT-A2-24 provides 24 months of manufacturing-defect coverage for the
Aurora Hub A2. The Lumen Sensor L1 has 12 months of manufacturing-defect coverage.
Coverage starts on the original purchase date and applies to the original purchaser.
An authorized replacement does not restart the original warranty period. Proof of
purchase and the device serial number are needed when opening a warranty claim.

## Included defects and exclusions
Covered defects include an internal power-board failure, a failed factory-installed
radio, and a display that stops functioning during normal use. Accidental liquid
damage is excluded. Cosmetic scratches and ordinary battery capacity loss are also
excluded. Damage caused by an unauthorized repair is not covered. A software setup
question by itself is a support request, not evidence of a manufacturing defect.
Support may request logs to distinguish a setup issue from a physical device failure.

## Remedy and service process
After a claim is approved, Northstar chooses repair or replacement. A refurbished
replacement may be used if it meets the original product specifications. Northstar
pays shipping both ways for an approved warranty claim. Customers should not ship a
device before receiving a service authorization. Back up personal settings before
service, since repair or replacement may erase settings stored on the device.

## Battery service and responsibility
For an Aurora Hub A2 outside warranty, battery replacement costs $39 including labor.
This battery service price does not include shipping. Northstar does not reimburse
data-recovery costs or third-party installation charges under this warranty. A normal
loss of battery endurance remains an exclusion even if the warranty period has not
ended. The service team checks the purchase date and diagnostic results before
confirming eligibility. Refund eligibility is governed separately by REF-30: a device
can be within its warranty period after its standard return window has already ended.
No extended-warranty purchase option is specified in this policy.
